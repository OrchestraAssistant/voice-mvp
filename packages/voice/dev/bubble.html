Bubble harness

A host page with no styles of its own, so the widget renders exactly as it does inside the shadow root. Defaults to mount="inline" so the panel can be driven by a selector; add ?mount=shadow for the real overlay path, ?bg=dark to catch a surface that is actually transparent, ?glow=1 to put the listening rim in the overlay alongside it, and ?trace=1 to record the resize and the top-layer churn below. Drive the panel with 1 and 2. /voice is proxied to the relay on localhost:3002; override with ?relay=.
